## 1. Problem & Vision

**Problem.** `[GROUNDED]` `loa-freeside` is still a thick vertical SaaS: `themes/sietch/src` alone is ~186k LOC carrying Discord/Telegram, product billing, and community APIs while most registry `*-api` cells are already external (`packages/freeside-registry/registry.yaml`; `grimoires/loa/reality/structure.md`). `[GROUNDED]` ADR-008 Current State already names this gap (`decisions/008-freeside-as-factory.md:211-227`).

**Operator thesis (2026-07-17).** The hub’s importance is **coordination + station + deploy platform**. Feature buildings should live at the edges. Bloat accumulated after adoption; several economic/agent surfaces are misunderstood (Spice Gate ≠ AI runtime; Redis budget ≠ credit ledger).

**Vision.** Thin the hub to platform substrate + network mesh. Extract (or finish extracting) stuck buildings. **Do not delete** load-bearing Spice Gate or live billing until a live belt replaces them. Honor ADR-007 firewall (no cross-domain PRs).
