## Problem

Four consumption surfaces converge on two dormant keystones. (1) **Shadow mode has a spine with
zero consumers**: the member-graph ledger (#316) is in-memory-only and nothing reads it, while
shadow-audit ships its OWN unwired ownership projection — two divergeable L2 truths, neither
chosen (EULER parallel-spine hazard). (2) **Shadow-audit-api is built and 100% dormant** (PR #387:
real chain adapters, Dockerfile, contract-parity-tested against the dashboard's dormant client) —
EULER-verified cut vertex feeding the sandwich, the probe leg, the wedge, and the ledger's first
consumer. (3) **Fulfilled orders' world manifests evaporate on redeploy** (live data-loss bug:
worlds-api PR #13 writes to ephemeral container FS). (4) **No external customer exists** —
KEEPER's sweep: both orders are operator rehearsals; the demo is the gate on demand ever becoming
observable; pythenians is the one code-declared outreach partner.
