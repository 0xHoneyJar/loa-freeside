## 1. THE HEXAGONAL MAP

A **cell** is a hexagon: a `freeside-X` building with a sealed interior. Cells never call each other directly — they touch only through **wax walls** (sealed Zod schemas + BeaconV3 `.well-known/beacon.json` declaring `consumes[]`/`publishes[]`). A **belt** is a directed wax-wall connection running `raw → derived → integrated → presented`. Direction is set by *data-semantic depth*, not by choice (ADR-008 §D-3: closer-to-raw publishes, closer-to-meaning consumes).

**The missing node — where it should live, and why (EULER + KRANZ).** The D1 spec noun is genuinely empty: `AccessDecisionRecord`, `HolderQualityBand`, `QualityBand` all return **zero grep hits** [observed]. But the hole is *two concepts collapsed into one*:

1. A **holder-quality BAND** (derived, can be stateless) — could be a *route on score-api*, not a new building.
2. An **AccessDecisionRecord** (per-member, persisted, policy-joined, *explainable*) — this is the genuinely new **L5-integration cell**.

The record's edge-signature *determines* where it lives. It must **persist** (the spec promises a *replayable audit* — "shows who is stale on snapshot-date… before you snapshot", #283). Persisted member-decisions **cannot** live in identity-api, because identity-api is explicitly **no-embed** — `getProfile` "joins inventory + score + codex via wallet[], **nothing downstream is stored in the spine**" (freeside-auth prd.md G-5 [observed]). It cannot stay in `apps/worker` either — that's the monolith interior the federation is trying to dissolve.

> **The verdict that surprises people:** the cut vertex is **D4, not identity-api.** By betweenness, D4 ≈ 1.0 (every audit belt funnels through the one join node). By *articulation* (removal disconnects the graph), D4 is still the answer: remove it and source→surface shatters; remove identity-api and only the *member-binding* belt severs — the minimal "who sold / whale concentration" audit (sonar→score→surface) **still flows.** So identity-api is the most-*connected* node but **not** a cut vertex for the minimal product. `sonar-api` is the DAG root (in-degree 0) — load-bearing as origin, but cutting a root *empties* a graph, it doesn't *disconnect* it.

**Honesty footnote (EULER).** n=5 cells is too small for a configuration-model null to have variance. The "D4 is the cut" finding is **not** a surprising-clustering claim — it is a direct structural fact (exactly one join node), verifiable by hand, which is what I did. At this scale the discipline is *structure-reading*, not significance-testing.

## 3. HEXAGONAL DESIGN, TAUGHT

Six reusable principles. These are the transferable discipline — they apply to any cell, not just this product.

### P1 — Ports before adapters.
Declare the *interface* (the wax wall) before any concrete implementation. `IEligibilityChecker` is declared (shadow-sync-job.ts:132 [observed]) — but its only implementation is a `vi.fn()` test mock. **A port with no adapter is a contract no one signed.** It compiles, it tests green against mocks, and it cannot run. *The lesson: a green test suite against mocks proves the port's shape, never the system's behavior.* When you see full test coverage and zero app imports, you are looking at a library, not a product.

### P2 — Belts run one way, and the direction is not yours to choose.
Data-semantic depth sets the arrow: `raw → derived → integrated → presented`. `sonar → score` is the only legal direction; `score → sonar` is *impossible* (you can't un-derive raw truth). When you're unsure which way an arrow points, ask "which side is closer to raw?" — that side publishes. **Bottleneck debugging = walk upstream on the belts.** [observed: the one WIRED edge respects this — score consumes sonar's belt-gateway.]

### P3 — The interior is I/O-free (brains-in-a-vat).
The Construct plane (hexagon interior) is *pure logic* — state machines, intent generators, no HTTP, no DB. The coexistence divergence logic *is* pure: `processMember` computes `hasRole !== eligible` against injected ports [observed]. That purity is *why* it can be hosted anywhere — and *why* it currently runs nowhere. **The interior's portability is a feature until you forget to give it a body.**

### P4 — A cut vertex needs a *contract*, not a *dependency*.
The most load-bearing node (here, D4) is where everything converges — so it must publish a *sealed schema* the whole graph can rely on, not reach into a neighbor's plumbing. **The anti-pattern, named precisely:** the member graph lives in `apps/worker/src/data/schema.ts:103` — a `profiles` table with `{communityId, discordId, telegramId, walletAddress → tier, currentRank, activityScore, convictionScore}` and `discordUnique`/`telegramUnique` constraints [observed]. Any concrete `getVerifiedWallet` must **puncture that monolith interior** instead of terminating at a published port. That's a belt connecting to plumbing, not to a wax wall — the exact thing the hexagon forbids.

### P5 — When two cells disagree on a noun, you have a wax-wall breach, not a bug.
The breach here is worse than location. **Two `EligibilityRule` types exist and are structurally incompatible** [observed]:
- *Port:* `IEligibilityChecker.checkEligibility(rules: EligibilityRule[], walletAddress)` → `ArrakisEligibilityResult` with tier (shadow-sync-job.ts:132).
- *Worker:* `checkEligibility(ctx, request, rule, checker)`, `EligibilityRule{ruleId, minBalance: string, chainId: number}` → `{isEligible, balance, blockNumber}`, **no tier, no aggregation** (EligibilityRepository.ts:36-46 [observed]).

These two answers to "is this wallet eligible?" don't know about each other. **A product whose entire value is *detecting divergence between two systems' eligibility views* cannot itself ship two disagreeing eligibility computations.** That's not a code-quality nit — it's a credibility hole at the center of the wedge. *Reconcile the noun before you wire a third.*

### P6 — Design the missing port as a schema *first*.
The fix for a hole is not "build a service" — it's "write the published schema the hole must expose." The `AccessDecisionRecord` should be authored as a sealed Zod schema in `packages/protocol/` **before** any runtime, because the schema *is* the contract that lets the rest of the graph consume the new cell. *The persistence question (P-fork below) is answered at the schema layer: does the record carry a snapshot-block + signature (replayable) or not (recompute-only)?*

## 4. THE MECHANICS (GYGAX)

The system, read as a game. **The player who matters is the operator (the CM), not the member.** A gated community is a game the CM plays *against entropy* — members drift out of eligibility continuously, and the job is to keep the role-set congruent with the holder-set.

### Core loop — two nested loops, only one closes.

```
MEMBER loop (inner, CLOSES):   acquire token → verify wallet → receive role → access  [incumbents run this fine]
OPERATOR loop (outer, BREAKS): set rule → bot grants → [TIME PASSES] → ??? → role-set drifts from holder-set → ???
```

The break, stated as a feedback failure: **grant fires on a state *transition* (cheap, event-driven); revoke requires continuous *re-evaluation* (expensive, poll-driven).** Incumbents fire grant reliably and re-poll lazily or never → a **faucet with a clogged drain.** The role-set monotonically accretes relative to true holders. It never breaks loudly; it accumulates error you won't notice until month three. **The missing feedback edge IS** `const divergent = incumbentState.hasRole !== arrakisResult.eligible` (shadow-sync-job.ts:394 [observed]) — the operator's missing sink, instrumented as a read-only sensor.

### The stale-access leak — three channels, one is load-bearing.

| Channel | What escapes | Type | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Status dilution | the role's signal value | *stock* (a ratio nobody bleeds over) | chronic |
| Scarcity dilution | gated-channel exclusivity | *stock* | chronic |
| **Allocation theft** | **real money at a snapshot** | ***flow* at a discrete settlement event** | **acute, per-event** |

**The load-bearing one is allocation theft**, because it's the only channel that converts a *stock* of error (`S/N = 12%`, a shrug) into a *flow* of irreversible dollars (`S × A = 12 wallets × $400 = $4,800 of treasury to people who already sold`, a budget line with names attached). **The leak becomes load-bearing exactly at the moment it turns from a ratio into a dollar figure** — and that moment is the snapshot. The stale set is already computed (`false_positive` — shadow-sync-job.ts:420 [observed]); **the `× A` multiplication is unbuilt and is the single highest-leverage display element** [proposed].

### Shadow onboarding — why no-install + touch-nothing is correct *for this player*.

The CM's payoff matrix is **catastrophic-downside / marginal-upside**: a live Discord is a production system with no rollback; a bad bot strips roles publicly in front of their own members. A rational CM under that matrix *doesn't install* — that's correct play, not conservatism. Shadow mode **rewrites the matrix**: it splits one all-in switch bet into a sequence of zero-risk observations with information gain (a free-roll). [observed mechanics: `ShadowSyncJob` non-mutation invariant; `NamespacedRoleManager` "MUST NEVER touch incumbent roles"; `GlimpseManager` feature-gated previews; `ShadowPrediction` self-scoring.]

> **GYGAX's sharp push:** the proof loop scores *accuracy*, but "this member is still eligible" stays true for 14 days **trivially** — the base rate of *change* is low, so aggregate accuracy runs 95%+ for free. The signal the CM cares about is **accuracy on the divergent set** — when you flagged a stale wallet, were you right? If `calculateAccuracy` is raw agreement, the graduation gate is certifying "good at the easy no-change calls" as "ready to actuate the hard divergence calls." *This is the dangerous mis-certification.*

### Money-moment demo — anchor on the *next* snapshot.

```
INPUT:   chain + contract + reference/snapshot date (+ optional gating rule)
COMPUTE: for wallets holding the gated role — which still satisfy the rule at the reference date?
OUTPUT:  • stale set (S × A, NAMED wallets, in the drop's own token)   ← the screenshot that travels upward
         • missing set (now eligible, no role)
         • turnover + whale/concentration note
CTA:     "Map this to your Discord roles with a no-install Shadow Access Audit."
```

A generic "your roles are 12% stale" is a stock fact with no deadline (the CM bookmarks it). "Before your snapshot on Friday, 12 wallets worth $4,800 sold but still hold the role" is a **flow fact with a deadline the CM didn't set and can't move.** *The snapshot supplies the urgency the product doesn't have to manufacture* — vitamin vs painkiller, sold exactly when the headache is scheduled.

### Graduation ladder — trust escalates by removing guardrails only after evidence.

```
shadow ──14d & ≥95%──► parallel ──30d & ≥98%──► primary ──────► disabled (escape valve)
(read-only)            (namespaced actuation)    (gating authority)
```

[observed: `CoexistenceMode` = `shadow|parallel|primary|disabled` (coexistence.ts:187); gates `requirementsMet: shadowDays >= 14 && accuracy >= 0.95` then `>= 30 && >= 0.98` (shadow-sync-job.ts ~622-642).] The shape is **correct**: dual-keyed (time AND accuracy) resists both failure modes (time-alone graduates on a quiet period; accuracy-alone graduates on three lucky days). **Two unverified safety properties make the top rung reachable or not:** (a) are the *down*-edges (primary→shadow/disabled) as wired as the up-edges? — a CM won't climb to primary if they can't instantly un-take-over (reversibility-first); (b) does *parallel* mode **union** access (both bots grant → temporarily *wider* access, the opposite of the promise) or **shadow** it?

### The 2-band MVP — a more *defensible* mechanic than a numeric score.

Band A = **stale** (`hasRole && !eligible`). Band B = **missing** (`!hasRole && eligible`). Both already computed at coexistence.ts:79 [observed]. **A band is a claim the CM can verify in one click** ("balance is 0, sold on date X") — its authority comes from onchain ground truth, not Freeside's judgment. A numeric `convictionScore: 73` is an *opaque assertion the CM must vouch for* — they can't defend "why 73 not 68" to their team, and revoking on it makes them liable for a black box. **Binary classification has a bounded, legible error surface (two checkable failure modes); a score has an unbounded, illegible one (a continuum of arbitrary thresholds).** This is the system-design expression of Eileen's ratified Option C — *"Freeside does NOT claim a full scoring brain day one."*

> **GYGAX's trap warning:** `convictionScore` already rides along in `ShadowMemberState` (coexistence.ts, surfaced into the state at shadow-sync-job.ts:404 [observed]). It's *right there in the struct*, so the path of least resistance is to show it. **Don't** — an unauditable number next to an auditable band *contaminates the band's credibility.* The CM starts wondering if the band is also a guess.


**Critical path = Act 1 → 2 → 3.** The entire build is **one cell, one adapter, one route.** Identity-binding, parallel mode, the graduation ladder, and the numeric score are all the *second* cutover.

### The load-bearing risk handed forward (P-fork at Act 2)

**Two eligibility engines already exist and disagree on shape; a third built fresh = three answers to "is this wallet eligible?"** Resolve before Act 2 writes a line:
- **(a) build fresh** against belt-gateway (cleaner, but duplicates eligibility logic = a future divergence bug, the exact class the product sells against), or
- **(b) adapt the worker's `EligibilityRepository`** up to the port (reuses ScyllaDB caching + tenant context, but forces a rule-shape reconciliation: `minBalance: string` ↔ `minAmount: bigint`, no-tier ↔ tier).

KRANZ and GYGAX both lean **(b)** on the principle *you should not ship two answers to "is this wallet eligible."* This is the one place where "while I'm here" is forbidden and "decide the seam first" is mandatory.

### #283 strategy gates (surfaced, NOT decided — these gate implementation)

- **Beachhead ICP:** a community with a **scheduled token event** (drop date on the calendar) — *not* "communities with stale roles" (that's everyone, no urgency). The date supplies the urgency.
- **Money-moment demo:** "Before your snapshot on `<date>`, **N wallets worth $X** sold but still hold the role." The load-bearing display element is **S × A, named, in the drop's own token** — not the ratio.
- **WTP gate:** "Would the CM pay to run their **next** snapshot through this before committing it?" — testable *only* once Acts 1–3 make the audit runnable against one real beachhead contract. If `S × A` for the chosen beachhead is negligible, the *beachhead* is wrong (pick more turnover), not the product.

## 6. THE STRATEGIC READ

**Why the Shadow Access Audit is the ONE load-bearing first-consumer that cures deployed-but-unconsumed.**

The four cells are the cluster's **signature failure**: deployed/wired *before a live consumer* ("alive ≠ has data"). sonar, score, identity are live and underconsumed; worlds is deployed and unregistered. The cure for that pattern is never "ship more infrastructure" — it's **one load-bearing first consumer that exercises the cells through their real ports and shadow-reads before it graduates.**

The Shadow Access Audit is exactly that consumer, and uniquely so for three reasons:

1. **It exercises all the cells through their published ports** — sonar (raw holders via belt-gateway), score (rank/concentration), identity (wallet↔discord, at demo-2), worlds (gating rule) — joined by the new decision-cell. It is the first thing that *consumes* the federation rather than adding to it.

2. **The adoption mechanic is frictionless by construction** (GYGAX): no-install + touch-nothing + see-results-before-commit means the consumer can shadow-read against live cell data *without anyone betting their production Discord.* The cure pattern *requires* shadow-read-then-graduate, and this product **is** shadow-read-then-graduate as a UX.

3. **The leverage is concentrated, not scattered** (KRANZ): **two edges (one concrete `IEligibilityChecker` + one CM readout) light up five mechanics** (core-loop feedback, leak quantification, money-moment demo, 2-band output, graduation entry). The build is small; the surface it lights is large. That is the opposite of the usual situation where each feature needs its own build.

**The fractal observation** (GYGAX → KRANZ): the cluster's signature failure is *reproduced one level down inside D4 itself* — a fully-built sink instrument (the divergence engine) with **no intake pipe** (no concrete eligibility adapter) and **no readout gauge** (no CM surface). The coexistence adapter is deployed-but-unconsumed the same way the four cells it would consume are. **Building the host cell cures both levels at once:** it gives the hostless library a body, and it gives the four cells their first real consumer.

> **The honest framing.** The optimistic read of #283 — "4 cells + a built D4 + one missing D1 node" — understates the D4 situation (built as logic, hostless as runtime, two critical ports interface-only) and overstates the D1 node (it's two concepts, only one genuinely new). The accurate framing: **the wedge is one missing building** that hosts D4, owns the persisted AccessDecisionRecord, and exposes the Reconciliation Surface — consuming the four cells across their existing ports. **Build that one cell, resolve the eligibility seam to a single answer, surface the two bands with dollars attached — and one wired edge becomes eleven.**
