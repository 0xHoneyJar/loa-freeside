Left alone, on purpose

Role-ladder swatches

Community and partner brand colours in _data/roles-shared.ts. The existing .ds-role-chip relative-colour recipe already pulls any hue onto the register.

Project mark backgrounds

Third-party logos in _data/projects.ts keep their own colour. Retinting them would misrepresent the brand.

Type, spacing, radius, glow geometry

Plex Sans, the analytics density scale, square corners and the glow-elevation mechanism are unchanged — glow is dialled to a rim, since Freeside doesn't bloom.
