The two corrections

Actions sit on four axes

Availability and presentation are separate concerns. An operator standing on a Terrace page is entitled to open operations; the label reaching for Service vocabulary is not.

Role · capabilitywhether the action may be performed
Resource existencewhether it may be offered at all
Exposurethe specificity of its label and explanation
Destinationresolves its own exposure under the ceiling

Availability must not depend on exposure alone; presentation may be exposure-governed. An action carrying a minimum also carries a plain label, and the projector renders that instead of withdrawing the offer. Capability and existence still withdraw it.

A7 Action availability never depends on exposure alone

Every channel is a disclosure channel

Text, numerals, geometry, ordering, colour, animation, accessibility metadata and DOM structure all represent the same facts, and each must stay within the precision the level permits. A bar whose width is the reading states the reading, whatever the caption beside it says. Geometry is quantized, not exempt.

Presentation doctrine only. Unauthorized rows, cells, counts and exports must be filtered server-side, before the payload reaches the client. A row omitted from the DOM is still in the response if the server sent it.

Conformance · eight classes, thirty-seven checks

Every pack, every case

A · Structural validity — 7Duplicate declarations, unknown selector IDs, missing policy axes, missing required-field fallbacks, secret on a required field, actions ungated.
B · Fragment policy — 6Every fragment through the projector at every context, with no selector involved. The check the inline-construction design could not make bite.
C · Selector behaviour — 2Known contexts choose the expected IDs, and choose them deterministically.
D · Reachability — 3Every production fragment is selected somewhere; canaries never are; required copy is never blank.
E · Deliberate failure — 2A canary projected below its floor really does fall back, records the reason, and fails a verdict.
M · Mechanism — 7Exposure within the ceiling, state cannot breach it, access does not theme, silence never confirms, export separately authorized, agency matches role.
N · Non-visual leakage — 3ARIA, title, data-*, form values, geometry and metadata read off the view model rather than trusted in markup.
R · Roster pack — 7Row existence, cell sensitivity, action authorization, the export grant, and that a capability grant widens nothing unrelated.

Run headless with node templates/_doctrine/run-checks.js, which exits non-zero on any failure or guard suppression. The Conformance card renders the same audit; it does not define the cases or the rules.
