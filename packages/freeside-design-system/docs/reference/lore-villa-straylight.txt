The strictly private villa of Tessier-Ashpool, located at the "tip" of Freeside station. One can reach there climbing towards the narrow end. There is only one entry and every approach is guarded. Inside, there is zero gravity.

The interiors are top secret and aren't displayed in the Freeside wireframes.
