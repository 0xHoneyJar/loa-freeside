A major avenue on the inner surface of Freeside. It is a circumferential avenue, looping the station's midpoint, intersecting Desiderata Street at the middle.

A broad street between huge buildings and shops, resembling a deep slot or canyon. Its ends are concealed by subtle angles of the buildings. It has access to the station's customs and the first thing one sees is a branch of the Beautiful Girl coffee franchise. A window displays the season's Paris furs. Overhanging tiers and balconies have tumbling green masses of vegetation that filter the light of the artificial Lado-Acheson sun.

At the intersection of Verne and Desiderata, a kind of gulch is formed, with several casinos and bars.
