Access-tier chip — a Freeside guest or staff tier. Credit is the key.

```jsx
<TierBadge tier="villa" />
<TierBadge tier="straylight" glyph="✦" />
```

Tiers: `port` (free-port arrival), `promenade` (gardens & resort), `villa` (dynastic), `straylight` (authority/elite), `concierge` (staff/systems), `vip` (nightlife). Soft tint + tier-ink text + a solid dot, or pass `glyph` to replace the dot.
