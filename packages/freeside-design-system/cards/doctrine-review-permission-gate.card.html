Permission gate, six contexts

Every frame is the real Design Component from templates/permission-gate, handed one context and nothing else. The copy, the withheld note, the meter and the two action labels are whatever the projector returned. Badges and axis figures are computed here, not typed.

Silent

Do not confirm existence. The screen reads as though the destination is not a thing here — for resources whose existence sits above the asker's ceiling.

Abstract

Admit handling, not contents. Something is being attended to and it is not yours. No structure, no identifiers, no counting.

Explicit

Name the withheld layer. The asker already knows it exists, so the refusal states which, at what ceiling, and that it was recorded.

A presentation review. A frame that shows nothing proves the screen said nothing, not that the payload was filtered — server-side authorization is the boundary.
